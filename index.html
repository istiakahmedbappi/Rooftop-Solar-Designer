Solar panels

Change the size by mouse or touch. Drag a red dot (left or right side), a blue dot (front or back side), the green dot (height) or the orange dot to turn it to any angle. Each side moves on its own. For small changes use the + and − buttons below. Shift + scroll scales the whole obstacle.

Building (from the map)

Panel layout

Vertical (portrait) puts the long side of each panel up the slope. Horizontal (landscape) puts it along the row. "2 high" fits two panels one above the other on the same frame, so the rows get further apart. Changing this re-fills the roof (Undo brings the old layout back).

Normal setting is 0° = panels face south. Use a minus number for east, a plus number for west. Change it for the whole building when the roof is turned or south is not possible.

In Move mode, drag the orange dots to reshape the roof, or drag an obstacle to move it. After changing tilt, roof or obstacles, press Auto-fill. Auto-fill keeps 1 m from edges and out of noon winter shade, so it differs a little from manual placement.
